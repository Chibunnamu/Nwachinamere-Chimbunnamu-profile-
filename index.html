01 / About me

I started as a designer and grew into a Software engineer — so I understand both what's possible and what users need, and I close the gap between them. Six years later, I've shipped products that real people trust with their money, their homes and their taxes.

I'm a Product Designer and Full-Stack Engineer based in Nigeria. My work covers secure fintech, government revenue systems, e-commerce, nonprofit infrastructure and cross-platform mobile apps built with Flutter, published on both the App Store and Google Play.

Every project starts with the same question: what does the person on the other side of the screen need? I take products from research and wireframes to production code, and I'm now open to senior product design and software engineering roles at teams that care about craft.
